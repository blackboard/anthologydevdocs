## General Enquiries
There are many ways to get in touch with us:

- E-mail the Blackboard Developers Network Team: [developers@blackboard.com](mailto:developers@blackboard.com)
- E-mail the Blackboard Partner Team: [partners@blackboard.com](mailto:partners@blackboard.com)
- Post to our [discussion space](https://community.blackboard.com/developers)
- Join the [Blackboard Techies Slack channel.](https://join.slack.com/t/blackboardtechies/shared_invite/zt-21ayvh4af-FMtP3g~MTHY0aay6CvcZtw)
- Tell us in person at a conference
- Provide your feedback through one of our many users groups

## Security Vulnerability Disclosures
:::warning
Suspected vulnerabilities and security issues shall be submitted solely as described here. Do not file security reports
with Client Support, through the support portal, by email or by chat.
:::

The [Blackboard Trust Center](https://www.blackboard.com/trust-center/security) includes a Product Security page
which includes our _Vulnerability Management Commitment and Disclosure Policy_, guidance for those who want to perform 
supplemental testing of non-production instances of our products. and a link to our **Vulnerability Disclosure Program 
(VDP)**, which is our [submission process for suspected vulnerabilities](https://www.blackboard.com/trust-center/submit-a-vulnerability).

The VDP incorporates specific measures to protect the confidentiality of submissions while they are triaged and, where 
necessary, while vulnerabilities are addressed.

Note that the VDP includes specific Submission Guidelines which must be followed. Simply submitting the result of a 
scan without steps to reproduce is not sufficient.
